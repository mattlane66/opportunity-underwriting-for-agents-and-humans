# Reference-Class Forecasting

Use an outside view when analogous markets/products can constrain an otherwise optimistic inside-view forecast.

## Selection

Prefer **3–5 defensible analogs** over a thin table of 10. Justify similarity on the variables that actually drive adoption or economics.

Deliberately include failed, stalled, or weak analogs when observable. A reference class composed only of winners defeats the method.

## Useful comparison dimensions

Choose only decision-relevant dimensions, such as:

- buyer and use case;
- switching/implementation burden;
- price and budget source;
- distribution model;
- adoption rate/time to scale;
- retention or repeat behavior;
- regulatory friction;
- gross margin/capital intensity;
- eventual penetration/market size.

## Use

Treat the outcome distribution as a constraint, not proof. If the subject opportunity requires outcomes far outside the reference class, surface that explicitly and identify why the analogy may break.
