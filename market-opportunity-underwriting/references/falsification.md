# Falsification and Query Inversion

Adversarial research is mandatory. Generate searches designed to find worlds in which the opportunity thesis is wrong.

## Seed patterns

```text
"[category] startup shut down"
"[competitor] churn"
"[product] switched back"
"[problem] not worth paying for"
"[category] failed adoption"
"why we stopped using [category]"
"[competitor] layoffs customers revenue"
"[category] procurement barriers"
"[category] cancellation reasons"
"[solution] cheaper alternative"
"built internally instead of buying [category]"
"couldn't sell [category]"
```

## Falsification targets

Seek evidence that:

- problem frequency/intensity is overstated;
- customers tolerate current alternatives;
- WTP is below the business model's requirement;
- competitors already solve the problem adequately;
- adoption has failed or customers switched back;
- procurement/switching costs dominate benefit;
- regulatory restrictions narrow the market;
- acquisition economics are poor;
- substitutes are improving faster than the proposed solution;
- the published market definition includes irrelevant spend;
- historical growth is slowing or was price/inflation driven rather than real demand.

Record contradictory evidence and meaningful negative searches. Absence of a result is not evidence that the opposite is true.
