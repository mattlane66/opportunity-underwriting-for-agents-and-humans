# Growth and Diffusion

## CAGR

Use only when start/end values refer to the same coherent market definition.

`CAGR = (V_end / V_start)^(1/n) - 1`

Every reported CAGR must include:

- start value/year;
- end value/year;
- geography;
- market definition;
- currency;
- nominal vs real treatment;
- inflation/base year if values are real.

A published CAGR is evidence to inspect, not a forward forecast to copy.

## Driver decomposition

Prefer a driver model such as:

`Market revenue = eligible population × penetration × usage/frequency × price`

Separate population/customer growth, penetration, frequency, price, substitution, regulation, and technology effects when possible.

## Bass diffusion

The Bass model is a canonical new-product diffusion model, but it should be used only when its adoption assumptions fit and parameters can be responsibly informed. For novel products with no adoption history, the correct output may be a model structure plus unknown parameters, not a fitted curve.
