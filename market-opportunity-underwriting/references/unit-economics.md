# Unit Economics

Do not apply a generic SaaS template to every business.

## Measures when relevant

- ARPU / ACV / AOV / take rate / usage revenue
- gross margin
- contribution margin
- CAC
- activation / conversion
- retention / churn / repeat rate
- CAC payback
- LTV, preferably from observed cohorts
- implementation/service costs
- working capital/inventory/capital intensity
- refunds/defaults/transaction fees/support/compliance

## Unknowns

For unlaunched businesses, actual CAC, retention, conversion, and payback are commonly `NOT_KNOWABLE_FROM_DESK_RESEARCH`. Industry benchmarks may create a reference range but must not be relabeled as company estimates.

## Simplified LTV

If a simplified formula is used, state its assumptions explicitly. Do not present modeled steady-state retention or margin as observed cohort economics.
