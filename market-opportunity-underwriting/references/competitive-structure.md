# Competitive Structure

Competition analysis is not a logo grid. Its purpose is to explain whether attractive economics can persist and why the apparent gap exists.

## Map

- direct competitors;
- indirect substitutes;
- internal build / manual alternatives;
- likely incumbent entrants;
- channel or supplier gatekeepers.

## Investigate

Where material, examine:

- target customer and positioning;
- pricing/business model;
- distribution;
- switching costs;
- buyer/supplier power;
- incumbent bundling;
- network/data advantages;
- economies of scale;
- regulatory barriers;
- commoditization risk;
- likely response to entry.

Always answer:

> **If this opportunity is attractive, why have existing firms not already captured it?**

A credible “why now / why open” explanation is evidence. “No competitors” is not.
